## Deposit

<Steps>
<Step>
**Connect a NEAR wallet** in the app. Connecting only shares your account name; nothing is signed.
</Step>
<Step>
**Enter an amount and review it.** The app simulates the deposit against the contract first: how many shares you'll get, the 0.5% deposit fee, whether the vault's cap allows it. If the contract would refuse, you see why before your wallet opens.
</Step>
<Step>
**Sign one transaction.** It sends USDC to the vault with `ft_transfer_call`. The contract mints your shares in the same transaction. If it can't take the deposit (paused, over the cap, or the share price is stale), the USDC comes straight back.
</Step>
<Step>
**The agent puts it to work.** On its next runs it moves the USDC into the vault's Intents account, sends a third to Hyperliquid as collateral, opens the short, then buys the coin. About a minute on testnet, about 10 to 20 minutes on mainnet.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Withdraw

<Steps>
<Step>
**Choose how many shares.** The app shows what they're worth today after the 0.5% withdrawal fee.
</Step>
<Step>
**Sign one transaction:** `request_withdraw`. Your shares leave your balance and the payout joins a queue. You're paid in order.
</Step>
<Step>
**The agent frees the USDC.** From idle USDC if there's enough; otherwise it shrinks the short and sells some coin. Then it calls `fulfill_withdraw`: the contract prices your shares at that moment and asks NEAR's signers to sign a transfer to you.
</Step>
<Step>
**The payout lands.** The agent submits it right away. About 1 to 3 minutes on testnet, about 10 to 20 on mainnet. Your withdrawal in the app shows each step, with links to the signing and payout transactions.
</Step>
</Steps>

## If the agent is down

`execute_withdraw` has no caller check. Once a payout is signed, anyone can submit it, and it can only pay the person who asked. If a signed payout waits too long (10 minutes on testnet, 30 on mainnet), the app offers a **Review and finish** button that submits it from your wallet.

A payout that hasn't been signed yet does need the agent: someone has to free the USDC and ask for the signature. Your shares stay in the queue until then.

## Where your money is while you wait

| Moment | Where it is |
|---|---|
| Right after deposit | USDC in the vault contract, your shares already minted |
| A few runs later | Coin in the vault's NEAR Intents account, collateral and short on Hyperliquid |
| After `request_withdraw` | Still in the vault; your claim is queued and priced when it's paid |
| After payout | USDC in your wallet |
