After step 8 the vault held 0.00040764 nBTC against a 0.0004 BTC short: hedged, at about 2x leverage on Hyperliquid, with a share price of $0.9978 (the deposit fee and the first transfer's cost).

## What this proves

- A NEAR contract can own a Hyperliquid account and a NEAR Intents account through Chain Signatures, and run real money through both.
- 1Click accepts intents signed by NEAR's MPC network for the vault's address (`/v0/submit-intent`), both for swaps and for collateral to Hyperliquid.
- The whole cycle works with real money: deposit, swap, collateral, short, report, withdrawal.

## Why so small

The contract is a hackathon build and hasn't been audited, so the mainnet vault is capped at $100. The owner can raise the cap with `set_params` without redeploying.
