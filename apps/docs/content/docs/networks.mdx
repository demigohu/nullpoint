| Part | Testnet | Mainnet |
|---|---|---|
| Vault contract | `vault.nullpoint.testnet` | `vault.nullpoint.near` |
| Same contract code? | Yes | Yes |
| USDC | Mock USDC with an open mint (free from the app's account menu) | Native USDC on NEAR |
| Intents | `verifier.nullpoint.testnet`: copies `intents.near`'s interface, with real ERC-191 signature recovery, nonces and deadlines | `intents.near` |
| Swaps and prices | A mock 1Click in the agent: quotes in 1Click's exact format, priced off Hyperliquid testnet minus 0.3%, signed with a key the testnet vault trusts | 1Click, with 1Click's own key in the contract |
| Collateral to Hyperliquid | A mock solver wallet sends real Hyperliquid testnet USDC | 1Click |
| The short | Hyperliquid testnet (real) | Hyperliquid (real) |
| Signatures | `v1.signer-prod.testnet` (real MPC) | `v1.signer` (real MPC) |
| Agent | `keeper.nullpoint.testnet`, every 60 s | `agent.nullpoint.near`, every 10 min |
| Deposit cap | $1,000 | $100 |
| Biggest single swap | $100 | $50 |
| Collateral slippage limit | 3% | 5% (1Click's flat fee is larger relative to small transfers) |
| Dust allowance in the report check | 0.5 USDC | 1.5 USDC (Hyperliquid's 1 USDC fee on collateral pulls) |
| Share price recorded | every 10 min | every 30 min |

## What testnet can't show

- Real 1Click fills and fees. Mainnet shows them.
- Realistic funding. Hyperliquid testnet funding runs to hundreds of percent a year either way.
- Hyperliquid's 1 USDC fee on transfers to new addresses, found on mainnet ([Lessons from mainnet](/lessons)).

## What mainnet adds

The same code path with real money: 1Click's `/v0/submit-intent`, real nBTC in `intents.near`, a real short. [Mainnet proof](/mainnet) has the transactions.
